The FluentRead reading assistant contains a small, browser-safe adaptation of
the append-only event ledger and message projection idea from
packages/core/session/src/surface.ts. It is not the complete DeepSeek Harness
package and does not include its desktop host, bridge, plugin loader,
filesystem, subprocess, sandbox, credentials, or process execution features.
